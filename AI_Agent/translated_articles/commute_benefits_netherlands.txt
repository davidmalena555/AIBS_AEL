More people in the Netherlands are choosing to use bicycles or public transport more often for their work commute. Cities aim to reduce car usage to decrease traffic jams, improve air quality, and ensure safer streets.

Full-time workers appreciate riding bikes as a way to stay active. However, not everyone finds this practical. People who live far from their workplace or need to carry heavy items prefer driving their cars.

Public transportation also plays a crucial role. Trains, buses, and trams can transport many people at once, but some complain about delays, crowds, and high fares.
