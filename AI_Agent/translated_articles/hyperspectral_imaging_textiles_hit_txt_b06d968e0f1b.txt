The research project HIT develops automatic recognition of textile composition. Mixtures of fibers and existing additives make sorting and high-quality recycling difficult. Existing clothing labels do not always provide sufficient reliable information.

The team combines hyperspectral SWIR-cameras with AI. Reference samples are investigated; images and information about the composition form data for recognition models. The project works on a dataset, algorithms, and a prototype for classification of textiles on a conveyor belt.

The project page mentions preliminary possibilities for recognizing pure and mixed materials. Accuracy, noise reduction, and scaling are further being investigated. This pertains to textile recycling, not to a proven commercial system specifically for sports clothing. The source does not provide guaranteed savings or inventory improvement for a sports store.
