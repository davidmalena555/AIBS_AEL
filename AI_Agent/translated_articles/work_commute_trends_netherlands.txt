More people in the Netherlands are choosing to use bicycles or public transport more frequently for their work commute. Cities aim to reduce car traffic by doing so.

Cycling helps keep workers healthy; however, not everyone finds it practical due to living far from their jobs or needing to carry heavy items.

Public transportation such as trains, buses, and trams also plays a significant role. Many people use them at once, but sometimes they face delays, crowds, and high costs.

The Dutch government plans to continue investing in better travel options for the upcoming years. They are constructing new bike paths and improving stations. Their goal is to make travel easier, cleaner, and more attractive for everyone.
