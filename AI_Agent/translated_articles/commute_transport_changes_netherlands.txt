More people in the Netherlands are opting to use bikes or public transport for their daily commute to work. Cities aim to reduce car usage by decreasing traffic jams, improving air quality, and making streets safer.

Bicycles help keep workers active; however, not everyone finds them practical due to living far from their jobs or needing to carry heavy items.

Public transit also plays a crucial role. Trains, buses, and trams efficiently transport many people at once, but some passengers complain about delays, crowds, and high fares.

The Dutch government intends to continue investing in more sustainable modes of transportation over the next few years. They are constructing new bike paths and enhancing station facilities. The goal is to make travel easier, cleaner, and more attractive for everyone.
