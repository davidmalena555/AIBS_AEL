Jinjiang Kitworks Dispatch, issue 47 Jinjiang, Wednesday 11 June 2035

Dear Wei,

You will not believe how much has changed since you left for the agency in Shanghai. We used to dread the last week before the export orders for the autumn leagues shipped, boxes everywhere, nobody sure which size run would actually sell. That part is mostly gone now.

This morning the forecast tool already had tomorrow's picking list ready when I sat down, because it had seen the youth league registration numbers come in overnight and flagged three club kits that would run short before Friday. Two years ago that same tool would have missed it completely, since it only looked at last season's sales and had no idea league sign-ups even existed. Someone finally taught it to read the registration calendar, and it changed everything.

Not every number it gives me gets trusted blindly, though. Anything under our confidence line gets kicked straight to my screen for a manual check instead of ordering itself, and today that happened twice, once for a brand-new club with barely two months of order history. I had to go and actually ask around before approving that one, because the tool was honest enough to say it did not know yet.

A new client's roster list came in by email as well, full of player names and sizes, and before anyone could even think about uploading it anywhere, the system had already stripped the names out on our own machines. Nobody has to remember to do that by hand anymore, which is one less way for someone to make an expensive mistake.

I still sign off every custom badge order myself before it goes to print. A misplaced club crest is the kind of thing a customer never forgives, and that is still entirely a person's job, not a model's.

Still hard: a club with no sales history is still mostly a guess no matter what the tool says, every vendor still gets grilled about where our data actually sits before we sign anything, and half of us spent evenings last year just learning what a confidence score even means.

Come by if you are ever back in town, the coffee machine finally got replaced.

Lin
Operations lead, Jinjiang Kitworks Co., Ltd.
